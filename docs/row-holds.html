How to read it

1 · WHERE
The row
One price is three times heavier than its neighbours, on two or more bars. Price touches it and closes back out. That is where the fight is.
2 · WHO
BNB leaving
While that row holds, open interest falls: −37, −33, −262, −104. Positions are closing and being taken by whoever sits on the row.
3 · WHEN
The flip
Open interest turns positive (+73) while price lifts off the row. New money, same direction. That bar is the trade.

Stop goes under the row. Target twice the stop. If BNB turns negative again before the move starts, you leave — the absorber was not finished.
